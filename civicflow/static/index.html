Report a problem on your street

Describe a road, waste, drainage, lighting or safety problem. CivicFlow organises it and shows a team why it matters.

JPG, PNG or WEBP, up to 3 MB. The photo is stored with your report but is not analysed.

Do not include names, phone numbers or other personal details.

What this prototype is not

Your report is not sent to any government department, and nothing here is an official decision. Priority scores are illustrative. For emergencies, contact local emergency services.

What happens next

  1. You reportYou get a reference ID to track your own report.
  2. AI organises itCategory, severity and a first action are suggested. If no AI is configured, labelled rules are used instead.
  3. A team prioritisesPossibly related reports are grouped and the score is explained in plain language.
